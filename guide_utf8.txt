CHAPTER 01
THE COMIC UNIVERSE
Vision, Aesthetic DNA &amp; Design Philosophy

1.1  THE BIG IDEA — WHY COMIC + WEDDING?
WeddingSnap&apos;s Comic Universe theme smashes two worlds together: the emotional warmth of a South Indian wedding and the bold, kinetic energy of a comic book / video game UI. Think Batman meets Baraat. Marvel meets Mehendi. Every screen should feel like an action panel — bold borders, halftone textures, speech bubbles for captions, and POW! moments when a guest uploads a photo.

The result is NOT a generic &apos;fun&apos; design. It is a premium, intentional, culturally rooted comic aesthetic that makes guests feel like HEROES in the wedding story.

1.2  AESTHETIC DNA

COMIC BOOK PILLARS

Bold black ink outlines on every card and panel
Halftone dot pattern (Ben-Day dots) as background texture at 8% opacity
Speech bubbles for captions and comments
Action word panels: POW!, ZAP!, CLICK!, SNAP!, BOOM!
Panel-style grid layouts — like comic book pages
High-contrast yellow, red, blue colour blocking
Hand-lettering style fonts for headings
Starburst / explosion shapes for highlights and badges
VIDEO GAME PILLARS

XP bar for Bride vs Groom leaderboard
Achievement badges on uploads: &apos;FIRST UPLOAD!&apos; &apos;COMBO x3!&apos;
Pixel-art style reaction counters
Health-bar style progress indicators
Level-up animation on upload success
Controller / gamepad icon on the upload button
Score ticker in the stats section
Boss battle frame for the admin panel

1.3  THE GOLDEN RULE
💬 SENIOR DESIGNER
Every screen is a PANEL in the wedding comic book. The couple are the HERO DUO. Guests are SUPPORTING CHARACTERS. Reactions are POWER MOVES. The admin is the DIRECTOR behind the scenes. Never let a screen feel flat — every pixel should have comic book ENERGY.
